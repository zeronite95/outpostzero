Where the science comes from
  • Solar flares: each flare in the game is a real one from NASA’s DONKI space-weather records (date and class are real). This is an archived list built into the game, not a live feed. The radiation points are scaled for play.
  • Radiation per day: about 1.4 mSv on the Moon’s surface (Chang’e-4 lander measurements, published 2020), about 0.7 mSv on Mars (NASA Curiosity rover RAD instrument), and about 0.007 mSv on Earth. A mSv (millisievert) measures how much radiation your body absorbs.
  • Radio delay: about 1.3 seconds one way to the Moon, 4 to 24 minutes to Mars.
  • Dust storms: a 2018 Mars dust storm ended NASA’s Opportunity rover mission.
  • A sol is one day on the base. On Mars it lasts 24 hours 39 minutes.
  • Simplified for play: oxygen, food, power and crew numbers are made up to keep the game easy to learn.
